          <li>Sound confident but not overqualified</li>
          <li>Show willingness to learn and contribute</li>
          <li>Mention readiness for an interview or further discussion</li>
          <li>Avoid generic phrases and emotional language</li>
          <li>Keep it between <strong>45–70 words</strong></li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="prompt-block">
      <div class="prompt-label"><div class="dot"></div>Closing — Prompt B (Quick Summary)</div>
      <div class="prompt-body">
        Write a closing paragraph summarizing my key qualifications for the <span class="ph">[job title]</span> role and expressing my enthusiasm for potentially joining <span class="ph">[company name]</span>.
      </div>
    </div>

    <div class="prompt-block">
      <div class="prompt-label"><div class="dot"></div>Closing — Prompt C (Call to Action)</div>
      <div class="prompt-body">
        Write a final paragraph that expresses my excitement about the opportunity to bring my <span class="ph">[specific skills]</span> to <span class="ph">[company name]</span> and requests to schedule a time to discuss my application further.
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
