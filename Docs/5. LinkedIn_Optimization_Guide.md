        <div class="format-bar-row">
          <span class="format-bar-label">Long-form Articles</span>
          <div class="format-bar-track"><div class="format-bar-fill" style="width:10%"></div></div>
          <span class="format-bar-pct">10%</span>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>

    <ul class="guide-list">
      <li>Turn on Creator Mode → select <strong>5 niche hashtags</strong> that describe your content pillars.</li>
      <li>Post minimum <strong>3× per week.</strong> Tuesday–Thursday are peak engagement days.</li>
      <li>Contribute to <strong>Collaborative Articles</strong> weekly — earns 'Top Voice' badge and dramatically increases discoverability.</li>
      <li>Use <strong>3–5 hashtags</strong> per post. Targeting beats volume; LinkedIn penalized over-tagging in 2025.</li>
    </ul>

    <h2 class="section-title"><span class="num">09</span> LinkedIn SEO — Advanced Discoverability</h2>
    <p style="color:var(--text-secondary);margin-bottom:14px;">LinkedIn functions as a search engine. These techniques maximize visibility in LinkedIn Search, LinkedIn Recruiter, and Google — all three index your profile independently.</p>

    <div class="keyword-order">
      <div class="callout-label">Keyword Placement Priority Order</div>
      <div class="formula">① Headline → ② About (3× minimum) → ③ Experience job titles → ④ Skills → ⑤ Education &amp; Certifications</div>
    </div>

    <ul class="guide-list">
      <li>Research your top 10 keywords using <strong>LinkedIn Search autocomplete</strong> — these reflect actual recruiter queries.</li>
      <li>Set profile to <strong>Public visibility</strong> (Settings → Visibility). Private profiles are invisible to Google and external LinkedIn search.</li>
      <li>Complete every profile section — LinkedIn's <strong>'All-Star' status delivers 40% more views.</strong> Check your completeness score in your dashboard.</li>
      <li>Publish monthly <strong>LinkedIn Articles</strong> on your target keywords. Articles are indexed by Google, extending your SEO beyond LinkedIn.</li>
    </ul>
