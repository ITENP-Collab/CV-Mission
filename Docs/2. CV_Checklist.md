## B — Content Sections

### 7. Career Objective — 2–3 sentences, no more
- ✅ *"Final-year BIT student at TU skilled in Python and React, eager to contribute to a technology-driven team."*
- ❌ *"I want to learn and grow my career."* | 6-line paragraph | Mentions salary or personal goals
- 💡 Formula: `[Degree + Institution]` + `[Top 2–3 skills]` + `[Value you bring]`. Never start with *'I am'*.

---

### 8. Education — Most Recent First
- ✅ Bachelor of IT (BIT) | Tribhuvan University, Kathmandu | 2021–2025 (Expected) | GPA: 3.6/4.0
- ❌ SEE listed first | *'Tribhuban University'* | Year written as *'21–'25*
- 💡 Include GPA or %. Verify the spelling of your university name.

---

### 9. Skills — Two Groups: Technical and Soft
- ✅ `Python, React, MySQL, Git, Docker` / `Problem-Solving, Communication, Teamwork`
- ❌ MS Word, Internet, Email | `Python ★★★☆` | `JavaScript 80%` | All in one mixed list
- 💡 No ratings or bars. Only list skills you can confidently discuss in an interview.

---

### 10. Projects — Minimum 2 (Strongest Section for Freshers)
- ✅ *"Built attendance system in PHP + MySQL, reducing record errors by 90%. — github.com/raj/attend"*
- ❌ *"Worked on a website."* | *"Did a project for college."* | GitHub link returns 404
- 💡 Formula per bullet: `[Verb]` + `[What]` + `[Tool]` + `[Result]`. GitHub link only if the repo has real content.

---

### 11. Work / Internship — If None, Expand Projects Instead
- ✅ *"Developed 3 React components, cutting load time by 30%. — Deerwalk Services, Jun–Aug 2024"*
- ❌ *"Was responsible for coding."* | *"I helped the team."* | Duties listed, not achievements
- 💡 Past-tense action verb first. No *'I'*. 2–4 bullets per role. No experience = remove section entirely.

---

### 12. Certifications | Extra-Curriculars | Achievements
- ✅ Google IT Support Cert — Coursera — 2024
- ✅ Secretary, IT Club TU — 2023–2024
- ✅ Merit Scholarship — TU — 2022
- ❌ *"Did a Python course"* | *"Was in a club"* | *"Got a scholarship"* | Incomplete courses listed
- 💡 Format all three as: `Name — Organization — Year`. Remove any section entirely if you have nothing to list.

---

### 13. Languages
- ✅ Nepali — Native | English — Professional Working Proficiency | Hindi — Conversational
- ❌ *"English — 80%"* | *"English — Very Good"* | *"English — Fluent"* if you are not fluent
- 💡 Accepted levels only: **Native / Fluent / Professional Working Proficiency / Conversational / Basic**

---

### 14. Photo & References
- ✅ NO photo.
- ✅ References: Do NOT write *'References available upon request'* — remove the line entirely.
- ❌ Selfie or casual photo | Listing referees without their permission
- 💡 Only add a photo if the job posting explicitly requests it for international roles.

## C — Formatting, Grammar & Export

### 15. Font, Size & Spacing
- ✅ Font: Calibri, Arial, or Helvetica — one font throughout
- ✅ Body text: 10.5–11 pt | Headings: 13–15 pt | Your name: 22–24 pt Bold | Line spacing: 1.15
- ❌ 3 different fonts mixed | Body at 9 pt to squeeze content | Single line spacing (looks dense)
- 💡 Go to Paragraph settings → Line Spacing → set to 1.15. Apply to the entire document.

---

### 16. Layout & Length
- ✅ Max 2 pages for freshers. Clean single column for MNCs/abroad (ATS-safe). 1-inch margins.
- ❌ 4-page fresher CV | Margins reduced to 0.3 inch | Tables/columns in ATS-targeted CV
- 💡 No star ratings, no decorative borders, no text boxes. Two columns only for Nepal-only applications.

---

### 17. Bullets & Consistency
- ✅ Same bullet symbol `•` throughout. Each bullet starts with a unique past-tense action verb.
- ❌ Mixed symbols (`•` `–` `▶`) across sections | *'Developed'* repeated 4 times in a row
- 💡 Vary verbs: Developed, Designed, Optimised, Deployed, Collaborated, Delivered, Resolved…

---

### 18. Grammar & Language
- ✅ *"Developed a REST API using Node.js, reducing response time by 40%."*
- ❌ *"I was responsible for developing..."* | *"We worked on..."* | Any spelling mistake anywhere
- ➡️ Run full CV through Grammarly. Then read the entire CV out loud — ear catches what eyes miss.
- 💡 No I / my / we. Past tense for past work. Short, direct sentences only. No vague adjectives.

---

### 19. Section Order Check
- ✅ Name → Contact → Objective → Education → Skills → Projects → Experience → Certs → Extra-Curriculars → Languages
- ❌ Random section order | Contact info buried in middle | Skills listed after Certifications
- 💡 Section order signals professionalism. Stick to the standard order above for general CVs.

---

### 20. Final File & Export
- ✅ Saved as: `FirstName_LastName_CV.docx` e.g. `Rajendra_Shrestha_CV.docx`
- ❌ `cv.docx` | `my resume final.docx` | `CVFINAL_v3_USE_THIS.docx` | File over 5 MB
- ➡️ Click every link in the final file. Open on your phone — readable? Then submit.
- ⚠️ Ask one trusted person (senior, mentor, or Grammarly) to review before submitting.
