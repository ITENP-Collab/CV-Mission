  <!-- SECTION 1: ATS -->
  <div class="section">
    <div class="section-header">
      <div class="section-num">1</div>
      <div class="section-title">What is ATS?</div>
    </div>
    <div class="definition-box">
      <strong>ATS (Applicant Tracking System)</strong> is software that reads your CV before any human does. It scores your CV and filters it. If your CV does not pass, no recruiter will ever see it.
    </div>
    <table class="dos-donts">
      <thead><tr><th>✔ DO THIS</th><th>✘ DON'T DO THIS</th></tr></thead>
      <tbody>
        <tr>
          <td><span class="tick">✔</span> Use simple, clean formatting — ATS can read it easily</td>
          <td><span class="cross">✘</span> Use fancy designs, graphics, or tables — ATS cannot read them</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td><span class="tick">✔</span> Include exact keywords from the job description</td>
          <td><span class="cross">✘</span> Use different words with the same meaning — ATS won't match them</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td><span class="tick">✔</span> Save as .docx or simple text-based PDF</td>
          <td><span class="cross">✘</span> Save as image, scanned PDF, or photo of your CV</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td><span class="tick">✔</span> Use standard headings: Work Experience, Education, Skills</td>
          <td><span class="cross">✘</span> Use creative headings: 'My Journey', 'Things I Know'</td>
        </tr>
      </tbody>
    </table>
    <div class="example-box">
      <div class="ex-label">Example — Keyword Matching</div>
      <p style="margin-bottom:8px; font-size:13px;">Job says: <strong>"Python, machine learning, data analysis"</strong></p>
      <div class="ex-row"><span class="ex-bad">✘</span> <span>'I know programming, AI work, and data science'</span></div>
      <div class="ex-row"><span class="ex-good">✔</span> <span><code style="background:var(--green-light);padding:1px 6px;border-radius:4px;font-family:var(--mono)">Python │ Machine Learning │ Data Analysis</code></span></div>
      <p style="margin-top:8px; color:var(--gray-600); font-size:12.5px;">→ Always use the <strong>exact words</strong> from the job description.</p>
    </div>

        <tr>
          <td><span class="tick">✔</span> Led 4 developers to deliver the project on time</td>
          <td><span class="cross">✘</span> 'Attended meetings and did coding'</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td><span class="tick">✔</span> Use leadership bullets: led, coordinated, organized, mentored, presented, negotiated</td>
          <td><span class="cross">✘</span> Write generic lines like "participated actively"</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td><span class="tick">✔</span> Include internships, part-time work, and freelance projects</td>
          <td><span class="cross">✘</span> Leave the section blank because 'I have no real experience'</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td><span class="tick">✔</span> Include your prior IT company (if any): Deerwalk, Leapfrog, F1Soft, Yomari</td>
          <td><span class="cross">✘</span> Use vague roles with no company name or date</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td><span class="tick">✔</span> Use present tense for current roles and past tense for completed roles</td>
          <td><span class="cross">✘</span> Mix tense inconsistently</td>
        </tr>
      </tbody>
    </table>
    <div class="example-box">
      <div class="ex-label">Example — Weak vs Strong Bullet Points</div>
      <div class="ex-row"><span class="ex-bad">✘</span> <span>Worked on the company website</span></div>
      <div class="ex-row" style="margin-bottom:10px;"><span class="ex-good">✔</span> <span>Built an e-commerce site using React and Node.js, increasing online sales by 25%</span></div>
      <div class="ex-row"><span class="ex-bad">✘</span> <span>Did data entry tasks</span></div>
      <div class="ex-row" style="margin-bottom:10px;"><span class="ex-good">✔</span> <span>Cleaned 10,000+ records in Excel, reducing data errors by 30%</span></div>
      <div class="ex-row"><span class="ex-bad">✘</span> <span>Helped with the mobile app</span></div>
      <div class="ex-row"><span class="ex-good">✔</span> <span>Developed 3 new features in an Android app (Kotlin) used by 1,200 active users</span></div>
    </div>
    <div class="example-box">
      <div class="ex-label">Example — Full Work Experience Block</div>
      <div class="ex-mono">Junior Developer  │  Leapfrog Technology, Kathmandu  │  Jun 2023 – Dec 2023
• Built REST APIs in Node.js for a healthcare app used by 500+ users
• Fixed 20+ bugs in existing code, reducing app crashes by 15%
• Worked in an Agile team of 8 using Jira and GitHub

IT Intern  │  Nepal Telecom, Kathmandu  │  Jan 2023 – May 2023
• Supported network configuration for 10 office branches
• Documented 50+ network diagrams using Cisco Packet Tracer</div>
    </div>
    <div class="tip-box">
      <div class="tip-label">💡 Quick Tip — No IT Experience Yet?</div>
      <strong>Freelance:</strong> 'Built a website for a local restaurant in Pokhara'<br>
      <strong>University:</strong> Write your college projects, team projects<br>
      <strong>Part-time (waiter, shop):</strong> Highlight teamwork, responsibility, and communication
    </div>
  </div>

  <!-- SECTION 6: EDUCATION -->
  <div class="section">
    <div class="section-header">
      <div class="section-num">6</div>
      <div class="section-title">Education</div>
    </div>
    <p class="section-desc">Keep this short. Most recent degree goes first.</p>
    <table class="dos-donts">
      <thead><tr><th>✔ DO THIS</th><th>✘ DON'T DO THIS</th></tr></thead>
      <tbody>
        <tr>
          <td><span class="tick">✔</span> Degree, University, Year, Grade (if strong)</td>
          <td><span class="cross">✘</span> List every subject and module you ever studied</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td><span class="tick">✔</span> Mention GPA or percentage if above 75% or 3.5/4.0</td>
          <td><span class="cross">✘</span> Hide your grade if it is strong — always show it</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td><span class="tick">✔</span> List 4–5 relevant subjects only for your first job</td>
          <td><span class="cross">✘</span> Copy-paste the entire university curriculum</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td><span class="tick">✔</span> Add certifications: AWS, Cisco, Google, Microsoft</td>
          <td><span class="cross">✘</span> Put certifications randomly with no dates</td>
        </tr>
      </tbody>
    </table>
    <div class="example-box">
      <div class="ex-label">Example — Education Block — Nepal Student</div>
      <div class="ex-mono">BSc Computer Science & Information Technology (CSIT)
Tribhuvan University, Kathmandu  │  2019 – 2023  │  78%
Key Subjects: Data Structures, Web Technology, Database Systems, Networking

Higher Secondary (Science)
Budhanilkantha School, Kathmandu  │  2017 – 2019  │  GPA: 3.7/4.0</div>
    </div>
    <div class="example-box">
      <div class="ex-label">Example — Education Block — Abroad Application</div>
      <div class="ex-mono">MSc Information Technology
University of Queensland, Australia  │  Feb 2023 – Dec 2024  │  GPA: 6.2/7.0

BIT (Bachelor of Information Technology)
Pokhara University, Nepal  │  2018 – 2022  │  82%</div>

  </div>

  <!-- SECTION 7: SKILLS -->
  <div class="section">
    <div class="section-header">
      <div class="section-num">7</div>
      <div class="section-title">Skills Section</div>
    </div>
    <p class="section-desc">Group your skills by category. Be honest. Only list skills you can talk about in an interview.</p>
    <table class="dos-donts">
      <thead><tr><th>✔ DO THIS</th><th>✘ DON'T DO THIS</th></tr></thead>
      <tbody>
        <tr>
          <td><span class="tick">✔</span> Group by type: Languages │ Frameworks │ Tools │ Cloud</td>
          <td><span class="cross">✘</span> Write a random list mixing technical and soft skills</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td><span class="tick">✔</span> Be specific: Python (Django, FastAPI), Java (Spring Boot)</td>
          <td><span class="cross">✘</span> Write just 'Python' with no context</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td><span class="tick">✔</span> Be honest about level: Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced</td>
          <td><span class="cross">✘</span> Call yourself 'Expert' in everything — interviewers will test you</td>
        </tr>
      </tbody>
    </table>
    <div class="example-box">
      <div class="ex-label">Example — Skills — IT Student in Nepal</div>
      <div class="ex-mono">Programming:   Python (Intermediate), PHP (Intermediate), JavaScript (Beginner)
Frameworks:    Laravel, React.js, Django
Database:      MySQL, PostgreSQL, MongoDB
Tools:         Git, GitHub, VS Code, Postman, Figma
Networking:    CCNA-level (Routing, Switching, TCP/IP)
Languages:     Nepali (Native), English (Professional), Hindi (Conversational)</div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <!-- SECTION 8: PROJECTS -->
  <div class="section">
    <div class="section-header">
      <div class="section-num">8</div>
      <div class="section-title">Projects</div>
    </div>
    <p class="section-desc">Projects prove your skills. This section is very important for students and freshers who have little or no job experience.</p>
    <table class="dos-donts">
      <thead><tr><th>✔ DO THIS</th><th>✘ DON'T DO THIS</th></tr></thead>
      <tbody>
        <tr>
          <td><span class="tick">✔</span> Name the project and say what problem it solves</td>
          <td><span class="cross">✘</span> Write only 'Final Year Project' with no details</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td><span class="tick">✔</span> List the tools and technologies you used</td>
          <td><span class="cross">✘</span> Leave out what languages or tools you used</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td><span class="tick">✔</span> Show results: users, improvement %, test score</td>
          <td><span class="cross">✘</span> Describe only what the project is — not what you built or achieved</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td><span class="tick">✔</span> Add GitHub or live link if the project is clean</td>
          <td><span class="cross">✘</span> Add a GitHub link that is empty or has one commit</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td><span class="tick">✔</span> Include: college project, hackathon, freelance, personal build</td>
          <td><span class="cross">✘</span> Skip projects because 'they were just assignments'</td>
        </tr>
      </tbody>
    </table>
    <div class="example-box">
      <div class="ex-label">Example — Project Format + 3 Real Examples</div>
      <div class="ex-formula">Format: Project Name  │  Tech Used  │  GitHub link (optional)<br>1 sentence: What problem? What did you build? What result?</div>
      <div class="ex-row" style="margin-bottom:8px;"><span class="ex-good">✔</span><div><strong>Nepal Fresher:</strong><br><code style="font-family:var(--mono);font-size:12px;">E-Commerce Site  │  PHP, MySQL, Bootstrap  │  github.com/ram/shop</code><br>Built a full online shop for a Kathmandu business with cart and eSewa payment. Now handling 50+ orders per month.</div></div>
      <div class="ex-row" style="margin-bottom:8px;"><span class="ex-good">✔</span><div><strong>University Project:</strong><br><code style="font-family:var(--mono);font-size:12px;">Student Result System  │  Python, Django, PostgreSQL</code><br>Automated result entry for 300+ students. Cut manual work by 70%.</div></div>
      <div class="ex-row"><span class="ex-good">✔</span><div><strong>Abroad / Advanced:</strong><br><code style="font-family:var(--mono);font-size:12px;">Fraud Detection Model  │  Python, Scikit-learn, Pandas  │  github.com/aadit/fraud</code><br>ML model trained on 1 million+ transactions. 94% accuracy. Deployed on AWS Lambda.</div></div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <!-- SECTION 9: GAPS & HONESTY -->
  <div class="section">
    <div class="section-header">
      <div class="section-num">9</div>
      <div class="section-title">Employment Gaps &amp; Honesty</div>
    </div>
    <p class="section-desc">If you have a gap in your CV, don't hide it. Handle it simply and honestly.</p>
    <table class="dos-donts">
      <thead><tr><th>✔ DO THIS</th><th>✘ DON'T DO THIS</th></tr></thead>
      <tbody>
        <tr>
          <td><span class="tick">✔</span> Briefly explain gaps: 'Self-study', 'Family responsibilities'</td>
          <td><span class="cross">✘</span> Leave months or years unexplained — it raises red flags</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td><span class="tick">✔</span> Turn gaps into positives: note courses or projects done</td>
          <td><span class="cross">✘</span> Lie about dates or make up a job — companies verify everything</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td><span class="tick">✔</span> Use accurate dates (month and year)</td>
          <td><span class="cross">✘</span> Round to the year only to hide a short job or gap</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td><span class="tick">✔</span> 'Extended study period, 2021–2023' for exam re-attempts</td>
          <td><span class="cross">✘</span> Pretend gaps don't exist and hope no one notices</td>
        </tr>
      </tbody>
    </table>
    <div class="example-box">
      <div class="ex-label">Example — How to Handle a Gap</div>
      <p style="margin-bottom:10px;font-size:13px;"><strong>Situation:</strong> 6-month gap after graduation</p>
      <div class="ex-row" style="margin-bottom:10px;"><span class="ex-bad">✘</span> Leave dates blank or skip the period</div>
      <div class="ex-row" style="margin-bottom:8px;"><span class="ex-good">✔</span> <div><strong>Option 1:</strong><br><div class="ex-mono" style="margin-top:4px;">Self-Development Period (Jan 2023 – Jun 2023)
• Completed AWS Cloud Practitioner certification
• Built two personal projects (see GitHub portfolio)</div></div></div>
      <div class="ex-row"><span class="ex-good">✔</span> <div><strong>Option 2:</strong><br><div class="ex-mono" style="margin-top:4px;">Family Responsibility (Jan 2023 – Jun 2023)
• Managed family responsibilities during this period</div></div></div>
    </div>
    <div class="warn-box">
      <div class="warn-label">⚠️ Watch Out</div>
      <ul>
        <li>Never make up a company or fake a job — employers call to verify</li>
        <li>Never lie about your grade — universities confirm this</li>
        <li>Never claim a certification you don't have — interviewers will test you</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>

  <!-- SECTION 10: EXTRA ACTIVITIES -->
  <div class="section">
    <div class="section-header">
      <div class="section-num">10</div>
      <div class="section-title">Extra Activities &amp; Soft Skills</div>
    </div>
    <p class="section-desc">Don't just say 'I am a good communicator'. Show it. Use a real example or result.</p>
    <table class="dos-donts">
      <thead><tr><th>✔ DO THIS</th><th>✘ DON'T DO THIS</th></tr></thead>
      <tbody>
        <tr>
          <td><span class="tick">✔</span> Club president, event organizer — with what you achieved</td>
          <td><span class="cross">✘</span> 'Member of Computer Club' — no achievement shown</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td><span class="tick">✔</span> Quantify: 'Organised tech fest with 400+ students and 12 sponsors'</td>
          <td><span class="cross">✘</span> List hobbies like 'watching movies, listening to music'</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td><span class="tick">✔</span> Hackathon: '4th place, Deerwalk Hackathon 2023, 50 teams'</td>
          <td><span class="cross">✘</span> Skip a 4th place — it is still an achievement</td>
        </tr>
        <tr>
          <td><span class="tick">✔</span> Volunteering: 'Taught basic computing to 30 students in Sindhupalchok'</td>
          <td><span class="cross">✘</span> Include anything not relevant like party membership</td>
        </tr>
      </tbody>
    </table>
    <div class="example-box">
      <div class="ex-label">Example — Extra Activities Block</div>

Hackathon — 2nd Place  │  Deerwalk Innovate  │  2023
Built a real-time flood alert app for Nepal using IoT sensors and SMS</div>

      <div class="tip-label">💡 Quick Tips for Nepal Job Seekers</div>

      Follow up once (politely) after 1 week if you hear nothing back<br>
