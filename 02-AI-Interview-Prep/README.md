# 🚀 Interview Preparation With AI

Prepare for HR, Technical, and Real IT Interviews Using AI

Welcome to **Interview Preparation With AI**, a free, nonprofit career readiness workshop by **IT Education Nepal**, created under the **Mission 10,000 Skilled Engineers**.

This mission helps students, freshers, IT learners, job seekers, and career switchers prepare for modern IT interviews using AI tools, structured answer frameworks, mock interview practice, and real mentor guidance.

This is not only about memorizing interview questions.

This is about learning how to think clearly, explain your skills confidently, defend your CV, answer HR and technical questions, and use AI as your personal interview coach.

---

## 🎯 Mission Objective

Many learners are rejected not because they lack talent, but because they do not know how to present their skills, explain their projects, handle interview pressure, or prepare with the right system.

This workshop is designed to help learners move from confusion and rejection toward confidence and interview readiness.

**Goal:**  
Help Nepali learners prepare smarter, answer better, and become ready for Nepal and global IT job markets.

## 🧩 Core Frameworks Covered

### STAR Framework

**Situation, Task, Action, Result**

Best for behavioral interview questions.

Example use cases:

- Tell me about a challenge you faced
- Tell me about a time you solved a problem
- Tell me about a time you worked in a team

---

### SOAR Framework

**Situation, Obstacle, Action, Result**

Best for career stories, struggle based answers, and personal transformation examples.

---

### CAR Framework

**Challenge, Action, Result**

Best for short problem solving answers and troubleshooting stories.

---

### PREP Framework

**Point, Reason, Example, Point**

Best for clear communication, opinion based answers, and leadership style questions.

## 🧪 30 Day Practice Routine

Use this simple routine for consistent preparation.

### Daily Practice, 30 to 45 Minutes

- 10 minutes, review one job description
- 10 minutes, practice one HR question
- 15 minutes, practice one technical or scenario based question
- 5 minutes, ask AI to review your answer
- 5 minutes, rewrite your answer in your own voice

### Weekly Practice

- Complete one full mock interview
- Improve your CV and LinkedIn based on interview gaps
- Update your GitHub or project portfolio
- Record yourself answering questions
- Review clarity, confidence, structure, and technical accuracy
