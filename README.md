# CV-Mission
- **What Is the 7-Day CV Mission?**

The 7-Day CV Mission is a limited, free initiative by ITEducation Nepal where our mentor Jiwan Bhattarai personally reviews 150 student resumes over 7 focused days.

This is not a chatbot. This is not a template generator. This is not automation.

This is Jiwan Sir – sitting with your resume – thinking about your future.

- **Who is This CV Mission For?**

This mission is open to everyone – regardless of educational background, college, GPA, gender, or location in Nepal. Whether you are:

    * A final-year student preparing to enter the job market.
    * A fresh graduate struggling to get interview  calls.
    * A student from a rural area with strong skills but no career guidance.
    * A dropout who has learned on their own and needs professional validation.
    * A beginner who has never written a CV before.
    * If you carry the seed of ambition, this mission is for you.

- **Meet Your Mentor: Jiwan Bhattarai – Global IT Expert & Career Activist**

Jiwan Bhattarai is a Global IT Expert, Mentor, Career Guide, and Education Activist working across multiple countries and multi-vendor technologies – including Datacenter, Networking, Cloud, Cybersecurity, IT Consulting, and Mentorship.

He has walked long roads – across countries, across failures, and through challenges that once seemed impossible. And from that journey, he carries one truth he shares with every student he mentors:

“Your past does not define your future. Rise. Learn. Conquer.” 

Whether you are from a rural village or a city, whether you are a boy or a girl, whether you are a beginner, a dropout, or someone who has faced setbacks – Jiwan believes that everyone carries the seed of greatness. You just need the right guidance to bloom.

This CV Mission is Jiwan’s personal gift to the 10,000 Skilled Engineer Mission – because Nepal deserves engineers who can compete and lead on the global stage.

- **Live Sessons Already Running – Watch and Learn**

The 7-Day CV Mission has completed all three sessions and they are live in ITEducation Nepal's YouTube platform. n these sessions, Jiwan Sir walks through real student resumes, explains common mistakes, and demonstrates how a weak CV becomes a powerful one.

These sessions are open to all – watch them, share them, and learn from them.

- **This is More Than a Resume Review – This is a Movement**

Nepal is producing thousands of skilled engineers and IT graduates every year. The problem has never been talent. The problem has been presentation, direction, and access to the right guidance.

The 7-Day CV Mission is ITEducation Nepal’s commitment to solving that problem – one student at a time, one resume at a time.

150 students. 7 days. Zero cost. Pure impact.
